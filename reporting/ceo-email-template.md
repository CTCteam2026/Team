# Weekly CEO Email — Template
*Send every Wednesday after the working session. Replace [brackets]. Keep to one screen. Link the scorecard for detail; the email carries the meaning. RAG: 🟢 on track · 🟡 watch · 🔴 off track.*

---

**Subject:** CTC Website — Week [#] update: [one-line headline, e.g. "site speed up 22%, first schema live"]

Hi [CEO name],

**Where we are:** Week [#] of the website audit & redesign. Overall status: [🟢/🟡/🔴]. [One sentence on the big picture.]

**What moved this week**
- **Speed:** [e.g. mobile PageSpeed 52 → 71; LCP 4.1s → 2.8s]. [Why it matters in one clause.]
- **SEO visibility:** [e.g. impressions +14% WoW; "event management Long Beach" #12 → #6].
- **AI searchability:** [e.g. CTC now cited in Gemini for 2 of 15 tracked questions, up from 0; Organization + LocalBusiness schema live and validated].
- **Conversion:** [e.g. 3 RFP submissions vs. 1 last week].
- **Execution:** [e.g. 6 of 8 P0 items done; homepage hero copy approved].

**Why it matters**
[2–3 sentences translating the numbers into business impact — e.g. "Getting CTC into Gemini's answer box means buyers see us before they scroll to a single competitor. We're now cited for two of the questions our buyers actually ask."]

**What's next (by next Wednesday)**
- [Item 1 — owner]
- [Item 2 — owner]
- [Item 3 — owner]

**One risk / ask**
[e.g. "Need Cloudflare admin access to finish unblocking AI crawlers — this is the single biggest lever on the Gemini goal." or "None this week."]

Full numbers: [Scorecard link]

[Your name]

---

### Fill-in tips
- Lead the subject with the **best single number** this week.
- Every bullet = **a number + a delta**. No number, no bullet.
- "Why it matters" should always tie back to the three objectives: **speed, SEO, AI visibility**.
- The **ask** is where you unblock yourself — name access, decisions, or content you need from the CEO.
