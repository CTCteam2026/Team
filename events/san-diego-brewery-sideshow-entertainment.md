# San Diego Brewery Party — Sideshow Circus Entertainment

**Event:** CTC brewery party, San Diego
**Prepared:** 2026-08-13
**Vibe (per Lynn):** A **side-show circus** — strolling, spectacle-style performers who wow the crowd. **Not** a carnival with games, booths, or midway ticket stands.

---

## What "sideshow circus" means for this party

Lynn clarified she does **not** want carnival games (ring toss, dunk tank, prize booths). She wants the **turn-of-the-century circus sideshow** feeling: performers who roam the brewery, gather a crowd, and do jaw-dropping acts. Think spectacle and atmosphere, not activities guests "play."

The three acts she called out — and how they fit a brewery floor:

| Act | Why it works in a brewery | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 🤹 **Juggling act** | High-energy, works close-up around tables and taps | Strolling + a few "gather-round" sets |
| 🗡️ **Sword swallower** | The classic "did that really happen?!" sideshow moment | Short scheduled showcase sets (2–3x during the party) |
| 🔮 **Palm reader** | Intimate, keeps a line of guests engaged all night | Seated station, guests come to them |

**Nice add-ons if budget allows (same sideshow world):** fire eater / fire performer, stilt walker (great for a brewery's tall/warehouse space and photos), human blockhead, contortionist, glass walker.

---

## Recommended approach

The cleanest booking is to hire **one circus company** to cover the juggler + sword swallower (and any fire/stilt add-ons) in a single contract, then book a **dedicated fortune-teller service** for the palm reader. Two vendors, one invoice each, easy to coordinate.

- **One-stop circus** → covers juggler + sword swallower (+ fire/stilt) → **Cirque Quirk** (first choice) or **Twisted Orbit** (alternate)
- **Palm/fortune reader** → **Party Psychic** (first choice) or **Add a Psychic to Your Party** (alternate)
