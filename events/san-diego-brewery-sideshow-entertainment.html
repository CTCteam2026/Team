A Side-Show Circus, Not a Carnival

Strolling spectacle performers who gather a crowd and wow it — jugglers, a sword swallower, a palm reader. No games, no booths, no midway.

What Lynn actually wants: the turn-of-the-century circus sideshow feeling — performers who roam the brewery and put on jaw-dropping acts — not a carnival with ring-toss games or prize booths. Spectacle and atmosphere, not activities guests "play."
