Three things to decide

Utilization

Is every paid hour a billable hour?

Our $60 and $42 map almost exactly onto salary ÷ 2,080 hours for Event Manager and Event Coordinator in Southern California — $42 is a dead-on match. That basis assumes 100% utilization. At the 75% most agencies plan around, true cost is $82 and $56, and the P&L is understating cost by about a third. This one assumption decides whether we run 40% margins or single digits.

Billing

Our markup is roughly half the standard

We bill $90 for a manager hour that costs us $60 — a 1.5× multiplier, against an agency convention of 2.5–3×. Market billing for corporate event work runs $75–$250+/hr, senior planners above $150, with corporate commanding about 30% over social. Our $90 / $65 sits at the bottom of that range.

Overhead

$0.61 an hour is not overhead

It is 1% of a manager hour. Real agency overhead — rent, software, insurance, non-billable admin salaries, business development — runs 15–30% of direct labor. If $0.61 is a specific pass-through such as a software seat, fine. If it is meant to carry our true overhead, it is short by roughly an order of magnitude, and hiring an Admin Assistant makes it worse, since that salary is pure overhead unless we bill it out.

Why this matters right now. Moving cost rates to $60 / $42 and adding the overhead line already cost about six points of margin against our last four P&Ls — LCT fell from 41% to 35%, AFCI from 47% to 42%. The 2% admin fee returns about one. Holding historical margin means client rates move too.

Method. 2026 US medians from the salary aggregators below, carried forward 3.5% to 2027. Cost per hour = salary midpoint × 1.30 burden (payroll tax, benefits, PTO, insurance), divided by 2,080 paid hours or 1,560 billable hours. Southern California = national × 1.12.

Confidence. Aggregators disagree substantially — Event Coordinator came back anywhere from $50,890 to $63,569 depending on source and methodology. Treat every figure as the middle of a range, not a point. "Senior Event Manager" is not a standardized title; it is built from Meeting/Event Manager and the 75th percentile of Event Manager.
