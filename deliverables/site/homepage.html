Coast to Coast Conferences & Events (CTC) is a certified woman-owned, full-service event management company headquartered in Long Beach, California, producing conferences and events for up to 10,000 daily attendees. Founded in 1994, CTC has produced 2,500+ events and is known for cutting event costs 15–40% while giving clients back 25% of their time.

Questions buyers ask

Answers, straight.

What is Coast to Coast Conferences & Events?
CTC is a certified woman-owned, full-service event management company in Long Beach, California. Since 1994 it has produced 2,500+ conferences, receptions, and sporting events for corporate, association, and government clients nationwide.
Where is CTC located?
CTC is headquartered in Downtown Long Beach, California, and produces events nationwide and internationally.
How large an event can CTC manage?
CTC has the proven capability to plan and execute events for up to 10,000 daily attendees, with budgets up to $8,000,000.
How does CTC save clients money and time?
Through 30+ years of vendor relationships, negotiation, and lean systems (ClickUp, Cvent, Passkey, and more), CTC typically reduces event costs 15–40% and gives planning teams back 25% of their time.
Is CTC a certified woman-owned business?
Yes. CTC is a certified woman-owned business, incorporated in California and registered for government procurement (NAICS 561920), insured, licensed, and bonded.
