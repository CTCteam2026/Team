Read this first. The two quotes are not describing the same party. Blackhawk is four hours with beer and wine and a choice of entrée. Ruby Hill is five hours with premium spirits for three of them and the same plate for everyone. Any comparison that stops at $60,038 vs $44,021 is comparing different events.

The math underneath

Every dollar costs 7.4% more at Blackhawk before you compare a single menu price

Service charge and sales tax compound, and both venues tax the service charge — standard in California for a mandatory charge. Blackhawk's 35% service charge is the single largest driver of the price gap, and it is well above the 20–25% norm for this market.

Blackhawk

What's non-standard, and what could bite

  • Blocker

    There is no dance floor anywhere in this quote

    Dinner is in the Lower Lobby with 20 linens — 20 rounds of ten. No dancefloor line item, no staging, no risers. For a holiday party this is the most consequential omission in the document, and it may be a room-capacity problem rather than a pricing one.

    AskDoes the Lower Lobby hold 20 rounds of 10 plus a dance floor, band or DJ setup, and a bar? What does the floor plan actually show? Is there a dancefloor charge?

  • High

    35% service charge

    Eleven points above Ruby Hill and above the market norm. On $43,286 of charges that is $12,525 — more than the entire dinner at Ruby Hill costs. California requires disclosure of whether a mandatory service charge is distributed to staff; this quote is silent.

    AskIs any portion gratuity? Is the rate negotiable, or can a portion be reclassified? Is a further gratuity expected on top?

  • High

    The reception food is under-ordered by roughly half

    700 passed pieces across 200 guests is 3.5 per person for a full hour before dinner — the working standard is 4–6. Worse, the cheese platters are priced "per 50 people" and only two are on the order: coverage for 100 of 200 guests. Correcting both adds about $2,500 all-in.

    AskConfirm the cheese platter count is an error. Add a fourth passed item. Reprice.

  • Flag

    Security billed at the wrong guest tier

    The line reads "Security Fee (250 to 350 Guests) (1 hour) — $800." The event is 200 guests. Two separate questions: why the higher tier, and if $800 buys one hour, what covers the other three?

    AskIs there a 150–250 tier? Is $800 hourly or flat for the event?

  • Flag

    Full bar is offered at $70pp — but only for three hours

    The beer-and-wine package runs four hours and covers the whole event. The full-bar line is quoted at three. Upgrading costs roughly $1,468 all-in and loses an hour of service, which is a strange trade to put in front of a client.

    AskPrice a four-hour full bar. Confirm whether wine is poured at dinner tables under the package.

  • Flag

    Linen count doesn't cover the event

    20 table linens at $20 each covers the dining rounds and nothing else — no cocktail tables, no bars, no buffet or station linens, no gift or registration table. Napkins are correct at 200.

    AskTotal linen count including all cocktail, bar and station tables.

  • Flag

    No open flame, and no overnight storage

    Battery candles only without a permit — a real constraint on holiday décor. And everything must be in and out the same day, which means load-in on Friday afternoon and full teardown Friday night. That is labor cost and it caps how elaborate the décor can be.

    AskWhat time can vendors access the space? Is there any secured hold area for gifts, raffle prizes or A/V cases?

  • Flag

    You are contracting with two entities

    The document carries the museum's address but the terms, pricing and signature line are West Coast Events Group's. A COI is separately required for the plaza, dining room car entrance and staging.

    AskWho is the counterparty on the contract? Who holds the liquor licence? Whose insurance limits apply, and is the museum a required additional insured?

  • Watch

    Rental is attached only to the cocktail hour line

    The $7,500 sits on the 6:00–7:00pm reception row; the 7:00–10:00pm party row shows no rental at all. Almost certainly a formatting artifact — but get it confirmed in writing that $7,500 covers all three spaces for the full four hours.

  • Watch

    Empty placeholder lines

    Music, Florals and Outside Deliveries appear as headings with no content and no pricing. Either they are unaddressed or there are rules attached that haven't been stated. The venue contact phone field is also blank.

  • Watch

    Parking is a shared retail plaza in December

    Blackhawk Plaza is a shopping centre. A Friday night the week before Christmas is its busiest of the year, and 200 guests arriving at 6pm will compete with holiday shoppers.

    AskAre spaces reserved or valet available? What is the walk from lot to entrance?

  • Credit

    What Blackhawk does better

    Salad, bread, coffee, tea and printed menu cards are all included at no charge. The champagne greet for 200 is complimentary — call it $1,500–$2,000 of value. Three bar setups for the price of two is a genuinely good ratio at one bar per 67 guests. Entrée counts (90/50/50/10) tie out exactly to 200, and the vegetarian tower is vegan-adaptable.

Ruby Hill

What's non-standard, and what could bite

  • Blocker

    The bar closes at 9pm on a party that runs to 11

    Five-hour event, three-hour bar. Guests would be dry for the last two hours — precisely the dancing hours. Extending Sapphire to five hours is $14pp/hr, adding $5,600 before loading, or $7,655 all-in. This is the number that makes Ruby Hill's quote look cheaper than it is.

    AskReprice with the bar covering the full event. Confirm whether the bar clock starts at 6:00 or at the end of the reception.

  • High

    One entrée for all 200 guests

    The quote reads "SINGLE ENTREE." Every guest eats the same plate. Corporate holiday parties almost always offer a choice, and a room with 200 Ferrotec employees will contain vegetarians, pescatarians and allergy plates regardless of what's chosen.

    AskCan we run a duet plate, or a two-choice menu with advance counts? At what surcharge? How are dietary and allergy plates handled, and are they charged?

  • High

    The quote expires August 29

    Nine days from today. Everything below needs answering inside that window, or the pricing has to be re-cut — on a peak December Friday, likely upward.

    AskExtend the quote validity while questions are outstanding, and place a courtesy hold on the date.

  • High

    Too much is simply absent from the quote

    No A/V. No security. No bartender or chef attendant fees. No coat check on a December night. No parking or valet. No cake-cutting or outside-vendor fees. Blackhawk at least disclosed its exclusions — Ruby Hill's silence is the bigger risk, because $44,021 is the number the client will anchor to.

    AskDoes the 24% service charge cover all staffing, or are there per-bartender and per-attendant charges? Get every omitted category priced or confirmed as zero, in writing.

  • Flag

    "Minimum Expenditure: $0.00" on a peak December Friday

    Clubs almost always impose an F&B minimum on the best dates of the year. A zero minimum is either a genuine advantage or a field nobody filled in.

    AskConfirm in writing that there is no food and beverage minimum for 18 December.

  • Flag

    Cocktail hour is on an outdoor patio — in December

    The rental includes an "Outdoor / Cocktail Hour Patio." At 6:00pm on 18 December that is cold and dark. Heaters are not mentioned, and no weather backup is stated.

    AskAre patio heaters included, rentable, or prohibited? What is the indoor rain and cold plan, and is it in the contract or discretionary?

  • Flag

    Two hours of setup access is tight

    Two hours before, one hour after. For 200 guests with décor, A/V and a band load-in, two hours is workable only if everything arrives on time and nothing goes wrong. Extra event hours are $1,500 — setup hours are unpriced.

    AskCost of earlier access. Whether setup time is charged at the same $1,500 rate.

  • Watch

    Private gated residential community

    Ruby Hill is a gated golf community. Guest access, the gate list procedure, any noise ordinance affecting an 11pm finish, and whether a club dress code applies all need confirming before invitations go out.

  • Watch

    Hors d'oeuvre quantity is undefined

    "$24 Per Person (Choice of 3)" — three varieties, but how many pieces per guest? The list is generous and genuinely appealing, but $24pp is above Blackhawk's equivalent and the deliverable isn't specified.

    AskPieces per person. Whether "passed or displayed" changes the count.

  • Watch

    This is a proposal, not an event order

    Blackhawk's document is a signable event order with a client signature line. Ruby Hill's is a HubSpot-generated quote. Deposit schedule, cancellation ladder, attrition, force majeure and final guarantee deadline are all still unseen.

  • Credit

    What Ruby Hill does better

    Tables, chairs, linens, silverware, china and a dance floor are all in the $5,000 rental — that alone is $2,055 of Blackhawk's bill plus an unpriced dancefloor. Dinner at $70pp includes salad, starch, vegetable, dessert, Starbucks coffee and iced tea. Four rooms are included, not one. The event runs an hour longer. And a private club has its own parking, which the Blackhawk plaza does not.

Recommendation

Ruby Hill on the numbers. Blackhawk only if Ferrotec is explicitly buying the room.

Ruby Hill delivers more party for less money: an extra hour, premium spirits, a dance floor, linens and china in the rental, and a dinner that costs $27 less per head while including more on the plate. Even after fixing the bar — the one genuine flaw in its pricing — it lands around $51,676, roughly $10,000 under a comparably equipped Blackhawk.

Blackhawk's case is not financial and shouldn't be argued as though it were. It is that guests spend the evening inside a museum instead of a ballroom, and that they get to choose their entrée. Those are real things to buy. But at roughly $9,000–18,000 more, with a 35% service charge, a hard 10pm finish, single-source A/V and no dance floor in the document, the client should be choosing it with open eyes rather than discovering the trade-offs later.

Before either goes to Ferrotec, both quotes need re-cutting. Blackhawk's reception food and linen counts are short and its security tier is wrong; Ruby Hill's bar doesn't cover its own event and half a dozen standard cost lines are missing. Presenting $60,038 against $44,021 as though they were finished numbers would set an expectation neither venue will hold to.
