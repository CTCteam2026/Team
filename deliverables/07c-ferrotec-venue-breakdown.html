Things to note

What to raise with each venue before signing

Everything flagged below comes from what the quote says, or from what it conspicuously doesn't. Neither document is finished.

Blackhawk Museum

  • Blocker

    No dance floor anywhere in the quote

    Dinner is 20 rounds of ten in the Lower Lobby. No dance floor, no staging, no risers. This may be a room-capacity problem rather than a pricing one — get the floor plan before anything else.

  • High

    35% service charge

    Eleven points above Ruby Hill and above the 20–25% market norm. On $43,286 of charges that's $12,525 — more than Ruby Hill's entire dinner costs. The quote doesn't say whether any of it reaches staff, which California requires you to disclose.

  • High

    Reception food short by roughly half

    Cheese platters are priced per 50 people and only two are ordered — coverage for 100 of 200 guests. Passed apps run 3.5 pieces per person against a 4–6 standard. Correcting both adds about $2,500 all-in.

  • Flag

    Security billed at the wrong guest tier

    "Security Fee (250 to 350 Guests) (1 hour) — $800" on a 200-guest event. Two questions: is there a lower tier, and if $800 buys one hour, what covers the other three?

  • Flag

    Full bar upgrade loses an hour

    Beer and wine runs four hours; the full-bar option runs three. Upgrading costs ~$1,468 all-in and shortens service. Ask for a four-hour full bar price, and confirm whether wine is poured at dinner tables.

  • Flag

    Linen count covers dining tables only

    20 linens is 20 rounds and nothing else — no cocktail tables, no bars, no stations, no gift or registration table. Napkins at 200 are correct.

  • Flag

    Two entities, one document

    The header carries the museum's address; the terms, pricing and signature line are West Coast Events Group's. Establish who you contract with, who holds the liquor licence, and what COI limits and additional insureds are required.

  • Flag

    Décor and logistics are constrained

    Battery candles only without a permit. Nothing stored overnight — in and out the same day, which means teardown Friday night and a cap on how elaborate the décor can be.

  • Watch

    A/V is locked to one vendor

    "OAVS, client to contract directly." Unavoidable, not in the $60,038, and not competitively bid. Get the quote now rather than after signing.

  • Watch

    Rental sits on the cocktail hour line only

    $7,500 appears on the 6–7pm row; the 7–10pm party row shows no rental. Almost certainly a formatting artifact, but get written confirmation it covers all three spaces for all four hours.

  • Watch

    Empty placeholder lines

    Music, Florals and Outside Deliveries appear as headings with nothing under them. Either unaddressed, or there are rules not yet stated. The venue phone field is blank too.

  • Watch

    Parking is a shopping centre in December

    Blackhawk Plaza on the Friday before Christmas is its busiest night of the year, and 200 guests arrive at 6pm. Ask about reserved spaces or valet.

  • Credit

    What it does well

    Complimentary champagne greet for 200. Salad, bread, coffee, tea and printed menus all included. Three bars at one per 67 guests. Entrée counts tie out exactly to 200 with a vegan-adaptable option. And the commercial paperwork is further along than Ruby Hill's.

The Club at Ruby Hill

  • Blocker

    The bar closes at 9pm on a party running to 11

    Five-hour event, three-hour bar. Two dry hours — precisely the dancing hours. Extending Sapphire across the full five costs $7,655 all-in. This single line is what makes the quote look cheaper than it is.

  • High

    One entrée for all 200 guests

    The quote reads "SINGLE ENTREE." Every guest eats the same plate. Ask whether a two-choice menu with advance counts or a duet plate is possible, and at what surcharge.

  • High

    No dietary or allergy policy stated

    Compounding the single entrée: a room of 200 Ferrotec employees will contain vegetarians, pescatarians and allergy plates whatever entrée is chosen. Nothing in the quote says how those are handled or whether they're charged.

  • High

    Six standard cost categories are missing

    No A/V, security, bartender or attendant fees, coat check, valet or parking anywhere in the document. Blackhawk at least disclosed its exclusions — this silence is the bigger risk, because $44,021 is the number the client will anchor to.

  • High

    Quote expires 29 August

    Everything on this list needs answering inside that window, or the pricing gets re-cut — on a peak December Friday, likely upward. Ask for an extension and a courtesy date hold now.

  • Flag

    "Minimum Expenditure: $0.00"

    Clubs almost always impose an F&B minimum on their best dates. A zero is either a real advantage or a field nobody filled in. Get it confirmed in writing.

  • Flag

    Outdoor cocktail hour — in December

    The rental includes an "Outdoor / Cocktail Hour Patio." At 6:00pm on 18 December that's cold and dark. No heaters mentioned, no weather backup stated. Get the indoor fallback into the contract rather than left to discretion.

  • Flag

    Two hours of setup is tight

    For 200 guests with décor, A/V and a band load-in, two hours works only if nothing goes wrong. Event extensions are priced at $1,500 — setup hours aren't priced at all.

  • Flag

    Hors d'oeuvre quantity undefined

    "$24 Per Person (Choice of 3)" specifies three varieties but not how many pieces each guest gets. The selection is genuinely strong, but at $32.81 loaded per guest the deliverable should be specified.

  • Watch

    Private gated residential community

    Guest access and gate list procedure, any noise ordinance affecting an 11pm finish, and whether a club dress code applies — all need confirming before invitations go out.

  • Watch

    This is a proposal, not a contract

    Deposit schedule, cancellation ladder, attrition, force majeure and the final guarantee deadline are all still unseen. Blackhawk's document is already signable; this one isn't.

  • Watch

    Does 24% cover all staffing?

    Neither per-bartender nor chef-attendant fees appear. Confirm the service charge is all-in, and whether any portion is gratuity.

  • Credit

    What it does well

    Dance floor, linens, china and silverware in the rental — that alone is $900 of Blackhawk's bill plus an unpriced floor. Dinner at $70 includes salad, starch, vegetable, dessert and coffee. Four spaces, not one. An hour longer. Premium spirits in the base quote with a fully transparent bar ladder. And its own parking.

Where this lands

Ruby Hill on the numbers; Blackhawk only if Ferrotec is explicitly buying the room. Ruby Hill is cheaper in every modeled scenario, runs an hour longer, and includes the dance floor. Blackhawk's case is that guests spend the evening in a museum and choose their own entrée — real things to buy, at a real cost of $9,000–18,000. The decision is legitimate either way; what isn't legitimate is presenting $60,038 against $44,021 as finished numbers, because neither is.
