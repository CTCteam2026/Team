Blackhawk or Ruby Hill:
the short version

Bottom line

Ruby Hill wins on money and on party. Blackhawk wins on the room.

Ruby Hill is cheaper in every scenario we modeled — including one where all 200 guests eat filet mignon and drink premium spirits for five hours. It also runs an hour longer and includes a dance floor. Blackhawk's case is atmosphere: a museum instead of a ballroom, and a choice of entrée. That's worth something — but it costs $9,000–18,000, and the client should choose it knowingly.

Before anything goes to Ferrotec

Neither quote is finished. Blackhawk's cheese platters cover 100 of 200 guests and there's no dance floor in the document. Ruby Hill's bar closes two hours before its own event ends, and six standard cost categories are missing. Presenting $60,038 against $44,021 as final numbers would set an expectation neither venue will hold.

Watch-outs

The six things most likely to cause a problem

  • Blackhawk

    No dance floor anywhere in the quote Blocker

    Dinner is 20 rounds of ten in the Lower Lobby. No dancefloor, no staging, no risers. This may be a room-capacity problem, not just a pricing one — get the floor plan.

  • Blackhawk

    Reception food is short by roughly half High

    Cheese platters are priced per 50 people and only two are ordered — coverage for 100 of 200 guests. Passed apps run 3.5 pieces per person against a 4–6 standard. Fixing both adds about $2,500.

  • Blackhawk

    Hard constraints on décor and logistics Flag

    Battery candles only. Nothing stored overnight — in and out the same day. Security billed at the 250–350 guest tier for a 200-guest event, and only "(1 hour)". Parking is a shared retail plaza on the busiest shopping Friday of the year.

  • Ruby Hill

    The bar closes at 9pm on a party running to 11 Blocker

    Two dry hours — exactly the dancing hours. Extending premium spirits across the full five hours costs $7,655 all-in. This is what makes the quote look cheaper than it is.

  • Ruby Hill

    One entrée for all 200 guests High

    The quote says "SINGLE ENTREE." Every guest eats the same plate, and there's no stated policy for vegetarian or allergy meals. Negotiate a two-choice menu or duet plate.

  • Ruby Hill

    Too much is simply missing High

    No A/V, security, bartender fees, coat check, valet or parking anywhere in the quote — and no contract terms at all. Blackhawk at least disclosed its exclusions. Also: a December cocktail hour on an outdoor patio with no heaters mentioned, and the quote expires 29 August.
